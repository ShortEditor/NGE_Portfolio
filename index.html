AN ORIGINAL PROMPT FRAMEWORK

The NGE
Layer Method™

Six layers for shaping an AI music prompt. Start with a feeling. Build toward the mix.

Open a layer to look inside ↘

01Emotion+

The feeling at the center. What should stay with the listener?

02Environment+

The space around the sound. Give the feeling somewhere to live.

03Story+

The movement from one moment to the next. Where does the piece take us?

04Sound design+

The instruments, textures and details that make that world audible.

05Vocal character+

The voice within the world: its tone, presence and delivery.

06Mix+

The relationships between the parts. Decide what comes forward and what leaves space.
